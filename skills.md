# Skills: WorkForWho

## Required Capabilities
1. **Natural Language Extraction:** Transforming unstructured voice/text into JSON.
2. **Clustering & Deduplication:** Identifying that "XYZ Const." and "XYZ Construction" are the same entity.
3. **Sentiment & Risk Scoring:** Quantifying qualitative reports into a 0-100 score.
4. **Multilingual Support:** Handling reports in regional languages (starting with English, expandable).

## Agent Personas
- **The Listener (Intake Agent):** Empathetic, focuses on extracting facts from emotional reports.
- **The Analyst (Intelligence Agent):** Skeptical, looks for patterns across multiple reports to identify systemic risks.

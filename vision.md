# Vision: WorkForWho

To become the "Glassdoor for the informal economy," powered by community intelligence and actionable AI.

## Core Pillars
1. **Radical Accessibility:** Voice-first and low-literacy-friendly intake.
2. **Actionable Intelligence:** Moving from "rants" to structured risk ratings.
3. **Worker Safety:** Anonymity by design to prevent retaliation.
4. **Community Trust:** Built for and by the workers, not the employers.

## Long-term Goal
Eliminate wage theft and exploitation in informal labor markets by making employer reputation transparent and searchable.

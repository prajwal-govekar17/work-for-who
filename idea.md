# Idea: WorkForWho (Local Worker Shield)

An AI-powered worker trust and employer risk intelligence platform designed for informal and non-technical workers.

## Problem
Informal labor markets (construction, domestic work, gig work) are highly fragmented. Workers often lack information about employers' reliability, payment history, and safety records. This leads to wage theft, unsafe conditions, and exploitation.

## Solution
A community-intelligence platform where workers can submit anonymous reports (text or voice). AI transforms these messy inputs into structured risk signals and aggregates them into a "Trust Score" for each employer.

## Value Proposition
- **For Workers:** Real-time warnings about bad actors and verification of reliable employers.
- **For Communities:** A collective memory that protects vulnerable members from systemic exploitation.
